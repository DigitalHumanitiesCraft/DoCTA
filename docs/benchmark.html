    HTR Benchmark

    Versioned prompt iterations of the VLM transcription, measured against reference pages and against the stability of repeated runs. It shows how much current vision-language models already read on Early New High German court records without any training. The page-by-page facsimile view lives in the viewer.

    Machine output Everything measured here is a first, fully automatic pass. No human revision has taken place yet; scholarly review turns these drafts into ground truth and edition text.

    Reference pages

    Inventory pages with a Transkribus reference transcription (status DONE). Character error rate (CER) as mean with range over the repetitions; fair = symmetrically normalized.

    Phenomenon pages without reference

    Account-book and inventory pages selected for script and layout phenomena. Measured by agreement between repeated runs, separately for word and number tokens.

    Protocol

    Prompts are versioned and frozen after their first run; every change is a new iteration with a documented rationale. Each condition runs several times, because identical requests vary even at temperature 0; a ranking from single runs would be noise.

    The references are themselves under scrutiny: Transkribus status DONE does not certify scholarly approval, and two reference pages turned out to be nearly empty and are excluded from the measurement. Known model failure modes are a modernization prior, smoothed balances that are arithmetically consistent but wrong, and silent line loss.

    How quality improves from here: adjudicated mini ground truth with the project team feeds the next prompt iteration (editor in the loop); domain lexica and controlled vocabularies (such as the Getty AAT for objects) constrain readings as post-processing rather than as prompt bias; and uncertain readings are explicitly marked so that human review starts where the model itself signals doubt.

    Raw data, prompts and runner: experiments/benchmark in the repository. Reference classes and approval protocol: Knowledge Vault, HTR-EVALUATION.
